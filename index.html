the science of nerve support

research in nerve health often focuses on addressing three specific enzymes: cyclooxygenase-2, prostaglandin e2, and matrix metalloproteinase-13. elevated levels of these enzymes are frequently studied in relation to nerve discomfort and excess oxidative stress on cells, tissues, and muscles. supporting the body's natural balance of these enzymes is a key focus in neuropathy research.
